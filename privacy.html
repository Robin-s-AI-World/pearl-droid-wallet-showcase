Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

1. No Data Collection

The PRL Wallet Android application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry. There are no trackers, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics SDKs embedded in the application.

2. Self-Custody by Design

Your private keys, seed phrases, and wallet data are stored exclusively on your device. They never leave your device, are never transmitted to any server, and are never accessible to the developers. You are the sole custodian of your cryptographic material.

3. Network Communications

The App communicates directly with the Pearl Blockchain peer-to-peer network and public block explorers. These connections are made to publicly available network endpoints and do not include any personally identifying information. The App does not proxy through developer-operated servers.

4. Website (prldroid-wallet.robin.mba)

The landing website is a static site hosted on a self-managed server. It does not use cookies, session tracking, or analytics. Standard nginx access logs may record IP addresses and request paths for server health and security purposes, but these logs are not used for profiling, marketing, or shared with third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

The App and Site do not integrate with third-party services that process personal data. The only external resources loaded by the website are:

  • Tailwind CSS CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com) — for styling
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — for typography
  • PDF.js (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — for the Blueprint flipbook viewer

These CDNs may log requests according to their own policies. No user-identifying data is sent to them by our site.

6. Referral Links

The Site may contain links to third-party cryptocurrency exchanges. These are referral links that may generate compensation. When you click a referral link, the third-party exchange may collect your IP address, browser fingerprint, or other data according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store that data. We are not affiliated with these exchanges beyond the referral arrangement.

7. Download Artifacts

When you download an APK or AAB from the Site, the filename and your IP address may appear in server access logs. These logs are used for bandwidth monitoring and security only and are not correlated with wallet activity or blockchain transactions.

8. Open Source

The complete source code of the PRL Wallet is available for audit at github.com/Robin-s-AI-World/prldroid-wallet. You are encouraged to review the code to verify the claims in this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the software or hosting infrastructure. Material changes will be noted in the release changelog. The current version is always available at this URL.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the project maintainers through the channels listed there.
